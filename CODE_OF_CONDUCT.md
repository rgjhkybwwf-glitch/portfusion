# Code of conduct

PortFusion is worked on in the open, and everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests and
reviews, is expected to keep it a place where people want to help.

## Expected

- Be courteous and patient, with newcomers above all.
- Criticise the code, not the person, and say why.
- Accept the maintainer's decision on what the project takes, and the reason given for it.
- Keep a real person's financial details out of every issue, pull request and example.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Repeated pressure after a decision has been made.

## Reporting

Report conduct privately, through this repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** on
GitHub, saying in the title that it is a conduct report. Only the maintainer reads it, and the
reporter's name is kept private.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove a comment, close a discussion, or block someone from the
project, temporarily or for good, depending on what happened.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
