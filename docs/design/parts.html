Light and shadow

Light comes from above. A surface is sunken, resting, raised or floating, and its shadow says which. Each shadow is a stack of layers, named here from first to last: a lit top edge, a hairline ring, a contact shadow close under it, and an ambient shadow that falls far and soft. In the dark the edge and the ring are a faint white, and the shadows fall deeper.

The parts, each in its states

A part has one rule in look.css, under the class named here, and each of its states is in that rule. The pages are made of these and nothing else. A state the pointer or the keyboard gives is drawn here by data-as, through the same rule; move the pointer over a part, or press Tab, to see the real thing. Under each part are its measures and what it moves, read from its rule.
